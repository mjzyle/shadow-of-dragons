## Backstory

<div class="player-card" markdown="1">

<p class="player-card-label">From the Player's Quill</p>

Eiravor was born in Pylossus in Thracia. As a half-elf in a town with a majority of humans, therefore, Arie was an outcast. At a young age she was bullied and ran in to the forest nearest the mountain. After days of attempting to surviving on her own, she was found by a pack of wolves. They raised her to hunt and and work as a pack or family. The one thing they didn’t offer was human mannerisms. 

As she got older she would wondering back to the village that ran her out. Blending into her surrounding, she would learn to be more humane, she heard all these stories of other lands and seaside adventures. She knew she had to explore more. Most of her pack had grown up so she started to explore different areas for longer durations of time. She found so many small villages and towns that were a collection of different races. The first time she felt normal in this kind of society.

Her adventures would become longer and longer where she didn’t go back to the pack, because of that, they were attacked. She came back and it was a massacre. Not hunting for sport, but for fun. Their bodies were everywhere, even the little ones were sprawled in the most horrid ways. She buried them all in a ritual she had heard about in one of them many villages she went to. After trying to hunt down the murderers, she eventually found herself returning to Phandalin to recenter herself. It kind of became her new pack. Though she mostly kept to herself, she became a local face in the crowd.
