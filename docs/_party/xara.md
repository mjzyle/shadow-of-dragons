## Backstory

<div class="player-card" markdown="1">

<p class="player-card-label">From the Player's Quill</p>

Xara's tale is one of heartbreak and desperation, a journey filled with both darkness and glimmers of hope. She lost the love of her life, a valiant warrior named Aiden, in a tragic battle against a powerful sorcerer. Devastated by grief, Xara could not bear the thought of living without him. Consumed by her sorrow, she sought out forbidden knowledge in the depths of the Dark Forest.  
  
In the shadowy heart of the forest, Xara encountered a reclusive and enigmatic sorcerer who promised to teach her the ancient art of dark magic, the kind that could defy even death itself. Desperate to reunite with her beloved Aiden, Xara delved into the forbidden teachings, sacrificing pieces of her own soul in exchange for the power to resurrect him.  
  
But as she delved deeper into the dark arts, Xara began to realize the true cost of her actions. The magic she wielded came with a terrible price, corrupting her spirit and staining her soul with darkness. Though she succeeded in bringing Aiden back from the realm of the dead, she could see the pain and anguish in his eyes, for he was not truly himself anymore.  
  
Haunted by guilt and remorse, Xara made a fateful decision to seek redemption. She knew she could never undo the damage wrought by her descent into darkness, but she could strive to make amends. Determined to find a way to cleanse herself of the taint of dark magic, she journeyed to Phandalin.

In Phandalin, Xara hoped to uncover new magic, one that could heal the wounds of her soul and restore balance to her shattered life. She sought out wise sages and learned scholars, seeking guidance and enlightenment on her path to redemption. And though the road ahead was fraught with peril and uncertainty, Xara faced it with courage and determination, knowing that only by confronting her past could she hope to forge a brighter future.
