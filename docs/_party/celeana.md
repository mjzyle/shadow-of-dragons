## Backstory

<div class="player-card" markdown="1">

<p class="player-card-label">From the Player's Quill</p>

In the heart of the ancient forests, amidst the whispering leaves and the gentle hum of nature, there lived a young wood elf named Celeana. From her earliest memories, she felt a deep connection to the natural world surrounding her. Born under the canopy of towering trees, she learned the language of the wind, the song of the birds, and the secrets hidden within the earth.

Celeana's childhood, however, was far from idyllic. As an orphaned wood elf, she found herself navigating the shadows of the forest alone. Without the guidance of parents or the protection of a community, she learned to survive by her wits and instincts. Celeana became adept at blending into the foliage, her small frame darting between the trees like a woodland sprite.

Living as an urchin in the forest presented its own challenges. Celeana scavenged for food, relying on her keen senses to locate edible plants and track game. She slept under the stars, finding shelter in the hollows of ancient trees or the nooks of hidden caves. But despite the hardships, she found solace in the embrace of nature, finding kinship among the creatures of the forest.

As she grew older, Celeana's connection to the natural world deepened, and she discovered within herself a latent power – the ability to commune with the spirits of the wild and harness the primal forces of nature. Guided by instinct and intuition, she began to unlock the ancient magic of the druids, learning to shape-shift, manipulate the elements, and draw strength from the land itself.

Though she remained a wanderer, drifting through the forests like a leaf on the wind, Celeana's heart yearned for something more. She longed to find her place in the world, to uncover the mysteries of her past, and to protect the beauty and balance of nature from those who sought to exploit it.

And so, with staff in hand and the whispers of the forest at her side, Celeana set forth on a journey into the wider world. Whether as a guardian of the wilderness, a protector of the innocent, or a seeker of truth, she knew that her destiny lay beyond the familiar shadows of the forest, waiting to be discovered amidst the ever-changing tapestry of the world.
