## Backstory

<div class="player-card" markdown="1">

<p class="player-card-label">From the Player's Quill</p>

Sherokk was a scout in the Great Gruumsh War. He fought on the side of the Deniers, a sect of orcs who tried to convince other orcs to set aside their hunger for death and bloodlust in the name of Gruumsh. An orphan of parents who perished under unknown circumstances, Sherokk was adopted by the Deniers—orcs as well as humans. After losing friends, Sherokk joined the war effort.

During the war, Sherokk was apprehended by enemy orcs, and in a move of cowardice, betrayed his battalion to the enemy. He was forced to watch the annihilation of his allies.

Haunted by the guilt of his actions, Sherokk became a monk, searching to forgive himself on this new path.
