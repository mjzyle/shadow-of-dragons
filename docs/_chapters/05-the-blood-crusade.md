*The hunt for Lady Calindra leads to a Dwarven city at war with a dangerous new threat from the Underdark.*

## Into the Stoneshield Mountains

A messenger from Pylossus arrived in Greenbriar carrying a letter from Matreus. The wizard had been tirelessly researching your earlier queries, looking for clues to help stop Lady Calindra and—more pressingly—her Orc associate, Anzad the Reckless.

First, Matreus shared that the Staff of Argonir was empowered by the gods through lost relics—material manifestations of their powers. He suggested these relics may be the key to weakening the staff and stopping Lady Calindra.

Matreus also explained that Anzad was using a curse of the Orc god Gruumsh to manipulate others—the very curse he cast on Xara and Sherokk to coerce you into locating the Staff of Argonir. However, the specific behavior Rakka described—Orcs behaving viciously, with little control of their minds—suggested another magic at play, far more sinister.

Finally, Matreus noted that the Underdark—a subterranean realm miles below the surface—had a single remaining entrance, guarded by a holy order of Dwarven paladins in their fortress-city: Helmmar.

With help from Rakka and Thalorian, you pieced together that Anzad was gathering Orcs—both willing and unwilling—in the Underdark. Suspecting that his actions were somehow tied to Lady Calindra's scheme, you traveled north to Helmmar by way of the Amberwood.

As you camped in the forest, Arie's magical protections caught an Orc scout skulking about. He revealed that a clan of Orcs had gathered nearby for a dark ritual—but not before taking a moment to taunt Rakka. He let her know that her brother has been eager to find her, and you all quickly learned that Rakka was not simply a misguided follower of Anzad: she was his sister.

At the ritual site, Anzad the Reckless called his followers to gather in the Underdark and fulfill their sacred mission. The Blood Crusade—a campaign of conquest in the name of Gruumsh One-Eye—is imminent. You saw Anzad wielding an ancient relic: a red and orange gemstone capable of dominating the will of the Orcs.

Though Anzad escaped, you disrupted the ritual and saved a young Elf scholar named Isidore. He had been traversing the mountains with his teacher, visiting ancient sites of the Elven goddess Selune, when Anzad's Orcs ambushed them. Though his teacher did not survive, Isidore thanked you for saving his life. You directed him to find safety in Greenbriar as you continued your journey northward, into the mountains.

## Helmmar

You arrived in Helmmar—a mighty city of stone in a vast cavern below the mountains—and entered the court of Queen Hilda Strongbrow. You learned that Anzad's Blood Crusade had gained ground in the Underdark, pushing back the Dwarven army as it marched ever closer to Helmmar. Expressing your intent to venture into the Underdark, the queen explained that the Bronze Steps were the only means of access. Given the war raging below, the passage was restricted to all but the city's holiest defenders: the Paladins of Helm.

Queen Hilda shared that a group of Orc spies had recently been apprehended near the Steps. They were being held in a prison camp under the protection of High Justicar Stonevein—head of the Paladins of Helm. Though the queen wished to see the spies executed, the High Justicar refused to carry out the order for unknown reasons. The queen said that if you could convince the High Justicar to reconsider, she would grant you access to the Bronze Steps.

You spent time exploring Helmmar, taking in the city's subterranean sights and unique Dwarven culture. In the Hammerhold district, Arie helped Forgemaster Ulrich repair his arcane forge using the elemental heart of Pyraxus. As thanks, Ulrich offered his services and a share of his smithing knowledge to help craft magical weapons.

Within the hustle of the city a young urchin snatched Celeana's Bag of Holding. You apprehended the youth—a clever Dwarven lad named Harry Fleetfoot—and gave him a stern lesson before continuing on your way. You purchased magic items from a particularly hairy Dwarf named Rurik in the Runestone Quarter. He offered you a generous discount as a trade for cuddles from Xara.

You ended your day in Old Town looking for a place to rest for the evening. Outside a rowdy tavern you met an inebriated Dwarf, who shared that he had been hearing strange voices in his walls at night. He wondered if the voices were connected with the disappearances of several orphaned children in the city. Hoping to help an old man in need, you spent the night in his house and investigated the voices. You quickly found their source: a band of orphans hiding in the cellar, led by none other than Harry Fleetfoot.

As his friends scattered into the woodwork, Harry begged you not to snatch him. You calmed him enough to learn that his friends had been disappearing in the streets near Helja's Cathedral—chief holy site and home of the Paladins of Helm. Using Harry as live bait, you managed to find a secret cellar where a coven of hags held the children as prisoners, teaching lessons about someone called the "Great Deceiver." You made short work of the hags and saved the children, gaining Harry's trust and his companionship on your adventures through the city.

## The Great Deceiver

With the children saved, you visited the Orc spies being held prisoner in Coalgut Row—a rundown quarter of the city. You realized the "spies" were a small band of dejected Orcs and Half-Orcs, ranging from grizzled old warriors to children. Their leader explained that they represented the last of the Deniers—a faction that fought in a bloody civil war amongst the Orcs: the Great Gruumsh War. The Deniers rejected the cruelty and violence of the ancient Orc god Gruumsh, fighting his most ardent followers in an attempt to free those Orcs subject to his harsh rule. They nearly succeeded in freeing their people when a devastating defeat at Cragmaw Hill destroyed their army. The last of the Deniers fled to the Underdark, where they remained in hiding for many years until Anzad raised Gruumsh's banner once again. They came to Helmmar with nowhere left to turn for aid and were promptly captured.

You visited High Justicar Stonevein at Helja's Cathedral, who said that he too suspected the "spies" were truly refugees escaping Anzad's crusade. Sensing your heroic nature, the High Justicar offered you an alternate deal without Queen Hilda's knowledge. Strange individuals were gathering in the ancient crypts beneath the cathedral, practicing forbidden magic in defiance of Helm, the Dwarven god of protection. If you would investigate the crypts and root out any signs of dark magic, the High Justicar would grant you covert access to the Bronze Steps, allowing you to make your final move against Anzad.

The crypts revealed the final resting places of paladins and members of Helmmar's royal family—as well as a secret lair protected by a prowling stone golem. You met a demilich who asked if you were servants of the mysterious Great Deceiver. When Sherokk replied that you were there to stop the Great Deceiver's plans, a brutal battle ensued in which you vanquished the demilich.

With the stone golem on high alert, you gathered what clues you could find in the lair. You learned that the Great Deceiver was an alias for Cyric, god of lies and deception, who took on the image of Gruumsh to manipulate the Orcs. Cyric was responsible for orchestrating the Great Gruumsh War and Anzad's Blood Crusade. You learned that the gem Anzad was using to control his Orc army was the Stone of Domination—a powerful relic of Cyric which allowed a chosen wielder to dominate the minds of others.

You entered the lair's innermost sanctum and found Rurik: the hairy magic merchant from the Runestone Quarter. Rurik revealed himself to be a prophet of Cyric and explained that your arrival at this place had been long foreseen. One amongst you, Rurik said, was destined to receive Cyric's blessing and claim the Stone of Domination as their own. He invited you to step further into the smoky sanctum and receive the Great Deceiver's blessing.

## Cragmaw Hill

The smoke transported you to a vision of the past. In the far north of the world, you met a camp of Deniers at Cragmaw Hill and learned that their general was the mother of Anzad and Rakka. The general was confident that their position on the hill was secure, though she expressed concern that her scouts had not returned. You knew one of these scouts by name: Sherokk.

Following the scouts' trail through the freezing wilds, you came upon a scene which Sherokk knew was coming. During the Great Gruumsh War, he had been captured by Gruumsh's army and, after hours of torture, revealed the location of the Denier army on Cragmaw Hill. What followed was the massacre that took the lives of most of the Deniers. Rakka, horrified that Sherokk's betrayal led to her mother's death and Anzad's ensuing rage, left to find her brother on her own, hoping to somehow convince him to end his crusade.

With Sherokk's guilt laid bare, the Great Deceiver revealed himself at last. The god of lies explained that this entire sequence of events—the Great Gruumsh War, Sherokk's betrayal, Anzad's grief, and now the Blood Crusade—had been orchestrated for the purpose of gathering a grand army solely for Sherokk. The Stone of Domination, he explained, latched onto the wielder's negative emotions—grief, anger, and guilt. Just as Anzad's grief and anger had been the means of his manipulation, Sherokk's guilt would make him a powerful instrument of the Great Deceiver's unknown plans.

Sherokk realized that he had something which Anzad did not: his friends. He chose to consult with everyone to help decide if he should accept Cyric's blessing. In the end, Sherokk claimed Cyric's blessing, granting the power needed to confront Anzad and claim the Stone of Domination.

Before leaving the lair, you killed Rurik—just as he had predicted—but not before granting his request for one last cuddle from Xara. With the Great Deceiver's disciples dispatched, the High Justicar led you to the Bronze Steps, instructing you to find the general leading the defense of Helmmar and do what you could to assist with the war effort.

## The Underdark

You descended the Bronze Steps into the depths of the Underdark. There you found a Dwarven fortress where General Flint Rexall led the fight against the Blood Crusade. The situation was dire. The Paladins of Helm were outmatched, many were injured, and they had little time and resources left to maintain their defense of the Blazing Valley.

General Rexall had recently sent two of his captains to investigate alternative flanks of attack, hoping to force a way through to Anzad's command post and take out the head of the Orc army. You chose to investigate the path of Captain Brackenbrook, journeying south through the Glittering Mire. In this festering subterranean swamp you discovered evidence of battle between Dwarves and Orcs.

While investigating an outpost at Worm's Rest, you were ambushed by a pack of Lizardfolk and their king, Reptilius. The Lizardfolk initially resisted committing to either side in the war, yet you managed to persuade Reptilius to aid in your fight against Anzad. He offered you a quest to prove your loyalty: take the weakest egg from his nest to the subterranean Black Sea, where it could be blessed in the waters by his reptilian gods.

You continued on Captain Brackenbrook's trail to a mystical Dryad, guarding the way through a range of fungal mountains. The Dryad desired payment for access to this path: the egg you had promised to bless for Reptilius. You chose to abandon this route, turning your attention north and keeping your bargain with the Lizard King.

You used your cunning and magic to navigate behind enemy lines and reach the shores of the Black Sea. The dark and mysterious waters blessed the egg of Reptilius, producing a curious young hatchling. The young Lizardfolk hurried back down the river, promising to bring word of the fulfilled bargain back to his king.
