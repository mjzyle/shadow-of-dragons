*The hunt for Lady Calindra leads to an ancient Dwarven city at war, where a dangerous new threat rises from the Underdark.*

## The Stoneshield Mountains

In the morning, a messenger from Pylossus brought a letter from Matreus. In it, the wizard detailed all he had researched regarding the earlier queries from the heroes. In particular, Matreus shared that the Staff of Argonir was empowered by the gods through lost relics—material manifestations of their powers. These relics may be the means of weakening the staff.

Matreus also explained that the warlock Anzad’s curse—the Eye of Gruumsh—is a painful spell used to control others. However, the behavior Rakka described of cursed Orcs behaving viciously suggests another magic at play alongside the Eye of Gruumsh. Anzad is using another power to manipulate these Orcs.

Finally, Matreus noted that the Underdark—a subterranean realm beneath the surface—has a single known entrance, guarded by an order of Dwarven paladins in their ancient city of Helmmar.

Armed with this knowledge, the heroes adventured north towards Helmmar. They traversed the Amberwood and made camp one day’s journey from the town, with Arie providing additional magical protection to the camp.

As Sherokk took the first watch, he was joined by Rakka just in time to spot a stranger skulking about the campsite. The two confronted the individual—revealed to be an Orc warrior. The sight of Rakka humored the Orc. He told the young Half-Orc that her brother has been looking for her.
