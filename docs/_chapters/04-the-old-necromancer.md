## The Fey Wilds

The Fey Crossing led to a strange forest in the heart of the Fey Wilds, filled with thorny black trees covered in blood red roses. As the heroes pushed on, they discovered carved symbols in the trees leading to a monument to Sune. At the foot of the monument were two long dead lovers, wrapped in an embrace.

Here Xara communed with Sune, learning that the goddess’s power—and her beauty—wanes each day Kelemvor’s debt goes unpaid. To save Sune, Xara was tasked with either fulfilling Kelemvor’s debt by finding and killing Aiden, or finding some other way to appease the God of the Dead.

## Castle Blackrose

The heroes arrived at a warped castle on a hill, where they were at last introduced to Sevrin Blackrose, the Old Necromancer. Blackrose assumes Xara has returned to finish their work in service of Sune. The party plays along to gain Blackrose’s trust.

Xara accompanied Blackrose to his laboratory—a chamber flanked by enormous crystal prisons containing captured souls. Blackrose harvested these souls to empower his great project, the Primus—a being of eternal beauty free from the shackles of both life and death. He sought to use the souls along with the mysterious powers of a black gemstone to empower the Primus, and he asked Xara to fetch and process a final batch of souls—souls gathered from slain druids defending Greenbriar—to complete his work. As Xara was sent to complete the work, Blackrose left to entertain another guest in his castle: Lady Calindra.

Reunited, Xara and the heroes consulted with Faustus and bartered for a broken crystal ball as a means of tracking Lady Calindra. The crystal ball, Faustus explained, can only be repaired using psionic magic.

The heroes then retrieved the souls for Blackrose and learned they must be refined and their memories extracted before fueling the Primus. With this information, the party devised a plan to share the unrefined souls with Blackrose as a means of sabotaging the Primus and gaining the upper hand in battle.

Further exploration revealed the Memory Bank—a vault containing all the memories of Blackrose’s captured souls. These memories are extracted and guarded by an imprisoned creature—a purple humanoid with writhing tentacles in the place of a mouth who speaks telepathically. The creature called itself Salazar. He explained he was banished to the Fey Wilds by a wizard, where he was captured by Blackrose and forced to assist with his experiments.

Salazar said the key to his release is likely on Blackrose’s person, and he offered to repair the party’s crystal ball in exchange for his freedom. When asked what he would do with his freedom, Salazar offered that he would seek vengeance for his imprisonment and feast upon those who stand against him.

With their plan decided, the heroes returned to Blackrose’s laboratory just in time to catch sight of Lady Calindra. The sorceress threatened Blackrose and extracted the name of the place where Halitrax was banished: Gehenna. Seeing no further threat from either Blackrose or Xara, Lady Calindra departed.

With Lady Calindra appeased, Blackrose was at least ready to complete work on the Primus—which he reveals to be Aiden, neither alive nor dead, caught in a comatose limbo. Xara enacted the party’s treachery, pouring the unrefined souls into Blackrose’s necromantic contraption. The raw power of the souls caused havoc throughout the laboratory. Detecting Xara’s betrayal, Blackrose used his magic gemstone in a last-ditch effort to channel the power of the souls into the Primus.

As battle ensued, Sherokk was teleported to the dungeons just in time to witness Blackrose’s fail-safe. A blazing fire rose slowly from the depths of the dungeon, intended to consume the entire castle and its residents.

Meanwhile, the other heroes defeated Blackrose and his servants, allowing Xara to share a final moment with Aiden—now an incomplete shell of his former self. Recognizing that what once was could not be restored, Xara used the power of Blackrose’s gemstone to grant Aiden a merciful death. She shared with her lover happy memories of their romance, including their wedding—glimpses of a time before darkness had crossed over Xara’s path. Through the power of the gemstone, Aiden finally crossed over into the afterlife, no longer a prisoner trapped between life and death.  

The heroes chose to leave the sullen Blackrose to his fate in the blazing castle, but not before confirming the information he shared with Lady Calindra: Halitrax and the dragons have been banished to a realm known as the Abyss.

With the threat of the Old Necromancer neutralized, the heroes finalized their deal with Salazar—using Blackrose’s gemstone to unlock the creature’s chains—and escaped in time to witness fall of Castle Blackrose, consumed in flames as the thousands of captured spirits fled into the night sky.

Back on the Mortal Plane, Salazar departed as the heroes returned to Greenbriar. Thalorian rewarded the heroes with sacred charms blessed by spirits of nature—though Celeana noted her former teacher was mysteriously avoidant and awkward towards her.

The team celebrated their victory with shopping in Greenbriar, meeting more of the ancient Druids who call the village home: Old Bramble (a slow-talking turtle and blacksmith) and Willowstep (a skittish doe who tends magically blessed fruits and vegetables in the community garden). The heroes settled in for the evening with more of Hearthhowl’s magic tea at the *Wolf and Warbler*, acquiring strange new abilities before settling in for a night of well-earned rest.
